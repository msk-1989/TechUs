Installments & Recurring Plans Are Always Paid Upfront
Every payment plan — Full Payment, 3 Installments, 6 Installments, or Monthly Recurring — is pre-paid: the

Quarterly, and Half-Yearly are simply different upfront period lengths a teacher can offer — the student always has
the choice of a single one-shot payment or a periodic upfront plan.
